# Ashford & Vale — Presentation Notes

Reference: [austin-estate-elevated.lovable.app](https://austin-estate-elevated.lovable.app/)  
Purpose: Steal *presentation lessons* for Realty of America · Office of Kevin Shoun — not the fake brand, stats, or visual identity.

## Steal (adapted)

| Pattern | Why it works | Our adaptation |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| Full-bleed hero, bottom-left type stack | Photography leads; copy never fights the image | Keep; text brand only in hero (no ROA SVG) |
| Region line above oversized headline | Quiet geographic authority | `Central Texas · Austin · Hill Country` + office line |
| Dual CTAs: portfolio + private consult | Primary = inventory, secondary = appointment | `View collection` + `Private consult` |
| Featured framed as “private collection” | Boutique curation > MLS dump | Eyebrow + quieter headline on featured strip |
| Soft listing labels on photo | Status without chrome/cards | Champagne hairline label, no badge pills |
| Firm / discretion long-form after inventory | Editorial trust after proof | Team teaser + About: discretion-first copy, dual CTAs |
| Closing CTA: appointment-only tone | Quiet luxury, no hard sell | “When you’re ready…” contact band |
| Generous section padding, serif display | Negative space = luxury | Keep Cormorant scale + `py-24`–`py-32` |

## Avoid

- Fake volume / estate / avg-price **stats strip** (or invent numbers)
- Brand name “Ashford & Vale”, Elena, license theater
- Putting a logo lockup in the hero
- Card grids with borders/shadows for listings
- Purple/glass “AI luxury” treatments
- Stat pills, floating badges, or promo stickers in first viewport
- Copying their cream/light palette — we stay ink navy + champagne

## Logo rule (ROA)

- **Header:** ROA SVG via `BrandLockup` (~220px)
- **Footer:** ROA SVG via `BrandLockup` (~260px)
- **Hero:** typography/copy only — never the SVG

## Tone to borrow (wording only)

Boutique, private, quiet, Central Texas. Prefer “collection / consult / discretion / by appointment” over “inventory / inquire / deals.”
