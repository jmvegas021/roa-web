Family-friendly restaurants in Salado, Texas means four addresses on a single walkable block of Main Street: a burger counter with hand-dipped milkshakes, a crêperie where kids can watch breakfast get made, an Italian gelato shop with a shaded patio, and a pizza-and-pasta room built for a menu every age at the table will eat. None requires a reservation, and all four are close enough together that a family can walk from one to the next without moving the car.

## Old Fashioned Burgers & Ice Cream

At 882 N. Main St., Old Fashioned Burgers & Ice Cream serves exactly what the name promises: fresh, old-fashioned burgers and hand-dipped milkshakes, according to the business's Visit Salado listing (Visit Salado, 2026). It is the most straightforward stop on this list — counter order, picnic-style seating, a menu built around a burger and a shake rather than a chef's tasting plan — which makes it the easiest first visit for a family testing out the village. One planning note worth building a trip around: the shop keeps limited days, open Tuesday and Friday from 7am to 5pm and Saturday from 11am to 6pm, closed Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday (Visit Salado, 2026).

## Marketplace Café & Crêperie

Inside the Shoppes on Main at 22 N. Main St., Marketplace Café & Crêperie cooks crepes to order on an open griddle, so kids can watch the whole process before a Nutella-and-fruit crepe or a savory spinach-and-cheese one lands on the table (Visit Salado, 2026). Co-owner Lara Tracy, who runs the café with her sister and mother, told [KCEN-TV](https://kcentv.com/article/features/spring-brunch-spot-in-salado/500-a6a1bd70-9aab-4518-aa48-d786dd1300dd) that "people just love our crepes" — popular enough on weekends that the kitchen can sell out by early afternoon on a Saturday. The café shares its building with more than two dozen boutique shops, so a slow breakfast here doubles as browsing time while a table clears.

## Vecchio Pallino

A few doors down at 106 N. Main St., Vecchio Pallino is an Italian artisanal gelateria turning out homemade seasonal flavors, with outdoor seating that makes it an easy stop between shops rather than a planned sit-down (Visit Salado, 2026). It is the simplest way to close out a Main Street afternoon with kids: no menu to negotiate, just a short walk to a bench and a cup of gelato in the shade.

## The Pizza Place

At 230 N. Main St., The Pizza Place keeps it to gourmet pizzas and pasta (Visit Salado, 2026) — the one meal on most kids' approved list, served in a sit-down room rather than a counter, which makes it the choice on this block for a family that wants to sit, order a full dinner, and not negotiate over what anyone will actually eat.

## What a walkable Main Street means for a relocating family

Four kitchens built for kids, inside a few blocks of each other, is not an accident of [Salado](/neighborhoods#salado)'s scale — it is what a one-mile village Main Street does on purpose. A family does not need a car between the burger counter and the gelato shop, and that same compactness is what makes a Saturday morning in Salado read differently from a Saturday morning in a subdivision built around a collector road. It is also a fair preview of daily life here: this same Main Street has a full morning of creek and shop browsing to offer beyond breakfast, and the [village's dinner-and-drinks addresses](/blog/where-to-eat-and-drink-in-salado-texas) cover the evening after the kids are in bed. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is the best kid-friendly restaurant in Salado, Texas?

Old Fashioned Burgers & Ice Cream is the simplest option for younger kids — a straightforward burger-and-milkshake counter with no reservation needed. The Pizza Place is the better pick for a sit-down family dinner.

### Is there a creperie in Salado, Texas?

Yes. Marketplace Café & Crêperie, inside the Shoppes on Main at 22 N. Main St., cooks sweet and savory crepes to order and is a popular weekend breakfast stop (Visit Salado, 2026).

### Can you walk between these restaurants on Main Street?

Yes. Old Fashioned Burgers & Ice Cream, Marketplace Café & Crêperie, Vecchio Pallino, and The Pizza Place all sit within a few blocks of each other on or just off Main Street, making it possible to eat, shop, and get dessert without moving the car.

### Is Old Fashioned Burgers & Ice Cream open every day?

No. It keeps limited hours — Tuesday and Friday from 7am to 5pm and Saturday from 11am to 6pm — and is closed Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, and Sunday, so it is worth checking the day of the week before planning a visit (Visit Salado, 2026).

### What else is there to do with kids in Salado besides eating?

Pace Park's shallow, spring-fed creek is a short walk from all four restaurants and is the village's other favorite stop for families, alongside the Main Street shops and galleries covered in our Things to Do in Salado guide.
