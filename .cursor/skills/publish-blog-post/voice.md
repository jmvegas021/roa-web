# Voice

Source: `design-system/MASTER.md` plus Kevin’s public bio. Do not invent a louder personality.

## Register

Restrained, confident, editorial. Quiet luxury — boutique, private, appointment-first. Million-dollar listing aesthetic: not suburban generic, not teal SaaS.

Write as the **Office of Kevin Shoun** at **Realty of America**, Central Texas. Kevin is a **real estate professional** (not Broker Associate, not “your realtor pal”).

From the site bio, keep this stance: precision, discretion, high-stakes timelines, military-forged calm, real estate as a long-term wealth instrument. Do not quote the bio verbatim in every post.

## Lexicon

Prefer: *collection / consult / discretion / by appointment / representation / counsel / office*.

Avoid: *inventory* (for homes), *inquire*, *deals*, *hot property*, *steal*, *unlock*, *dive in*, *game-changer*, *crush it*, *let’s get started*, emoji, hashtag walls.

Prefer *piece* or *article* in asides if needed; the public nav and index are **Blog** at `/blog`. In the post itself, just write the article — do not narrate that it is a “blog post”.

## Lead

Definition-first. Name the place or the decision in the first two sentences. No weather, no “whether you’re a first-time buyer or a seasoned investor”, no “in today’s market”.

Yes: “Salado and Belton sit eight miles apart on I-35 and solve different lives.”

No: “Buying a home is one of the biggest decisions you’ll ever make.”

## Proof

- Real citations only. Link them.
- No fabricated closing counts, average days on market, or “my clients often…” anecdotes.
- Existing site testimonials stay on marketing pages; do not mint new named clients.
- Stats / social proof, if used at all, must be real — never invent volume metrics.

## Place names

- **Fort Cavazos** (historical “Fort Hood” only when citing an old document).
- Markets: Salado, Belton, Temple, Georgetown, Harker Heights, Bell County, Williamson County, Central Texas.
- Creek-side Salado, Lake Belton, Temple’s medical hub, Georgetown’s square — character, not checklists.

## CTA

Dual spirit of the site: collection + private consult. In a blog post, one quiet close is enough:

- Work with Kevin → `/contact` or `/agents/kevin-shoun`
- See the collection → `/listings`

No countdown language. No “schedule your free consult today!”

## Title and meta

Titles read like a magazine deck, not a listicle. The primary keyword appears once, naturally. Descriptions are complete sentences (40–180 characters, aim ~155), including the keyword and a place name — not keyword strings. See [seo.md](seo.md).

## Anti-patterns

- Purple/indigo, cream+terracotta “AI luxury”, or teal SaaS tone in the prose
- Dense broadsheet / SEO doorway pages or keyword stuffing in title, slug, alt, or body
- Fake brokerage stats copied from demos
- First-person overclaim (“I’ve sold 400 homes in Salado”)
- Exclamation points in headlines
